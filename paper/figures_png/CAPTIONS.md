# Figure captions

All six figures are PNG at 300 dpi, copied unchanged from `paper/figures/` (vector PDFs are there too). They were made by `paper/make_figures.py` from stored results; input hashes are in `paper/figures/figure_sources.json`. G = Gaussian HMM, T = Student-t HMM, H = HSMM. "Baseline" = the same family fitted on the deseasonalised 5-minute return only.

**Figure 1 (`fig1_h1a_pauc_gain.png`). Detection gain from the volatility feature, H1(a).** Partial AUC (null alarm rate 0 to 0.5) of the feature model minus that of its returns-only baseline, on synthetic splices of stable test days with a planted variance change at a known minute. (a) Development: effects on the 22 confirmatory folds where H1(a) is evaluable, with the fold mean and a 95% percentile bootstrap interval of the mean (10,000 resamples; computed for this figure only; the protocol test is the fold-level block-bootstrap p). (b) Evaluation with the frozen models, 2019-01-02 to 2022-03-04 (LEGACY) and 2025-01-02 to 2026-10-12 (RAW), with 95% day-cluster bootstrap intervals (2,000 resamples). Evaluation periods were previously exposed; their results are reported as adaptive/exploratory effect sizes.

**Figure 2 (`fig2_fomc_hit_rates.png`). FOMC statements, H1(c) (secondary).** Share of scheduled FOMC statement releases followed by a confirmed alarm within 60 minutes, feature model vs returns-only baseline, at thresholds calibrated to 0.2 alarms per validation session. Development: 98 events, Wilson 95% intervals. Evaluation: 24 (LEGACY) and 14 (RAW) events; Wilson 95% intervals computed for this figure from the stored hit rates (the stored evaluation interval is for the difference only).

**Figure 3 (`fig3_detection_by_break_size.png`). Absolute detection by break size (post hoc, descriptive).** LEGACY development confirmatory folds, models at their calibrated thresholds; solid lines feature models, dashed lines returns-only baselines. (a) Share of splices with a planted variance increase detected within 60 minutes (first update of the alarm run after the onset, confirmation within 60 minutes), by variance ratio (variance, not volatility; 2,016 splices per point). (b) The same for variance decreases. (c) Median minutes from onset to confirmation for the hits in (a), with interquartile range.

**Figure 4 (`fig4_h3_gamma.png`). H3: next-day volatility of other markets after a SPY calm-to-stressed switch.** Coefficient γ of the break indicator in the regression of log next-day realised variance on own recent volatility controls, breaks vs matched placebo times. One-sided H0: γ ≤ 0; no test rejects (Holm over six tests). Filled markers: development, Gaussian HMM and HSMM; open triangles: evaluation, Gaussian HMM. Intervals (two-sided 95% week-block bootstrap, post hoc) exist only for development TLT and gold; lighter markers have no interval. Evaluation estimates are not reliable for TLT and gold because the random-time null control failed there.

**Figure 5 (`fig5_a30_timing.png`). Lead-lag of the cross-asset response (A30, descriptive).** For each Gaussian HMM calm-to-stressed break, the first 5-minute bar in [S − 30, T + 60] at which the asset's absolute cumulative return exceeds the 90th percentile of its placebo distribution, in minutes relative to S (the first update of the confirming SPY run). Distributions normalised within each period; n = breaks with an exceedance. Development: 600 breaks, 538 with a window; evaluation: 218 breaks, 201 with a window.

**Figure 6 (`fig6_seasonality.png`). Time-of-day pattern before and after seasonal adjustment.** Per-minute median over full 390-minute TRAIN sessions of the absolute 1-minute return and of 10-minute realised volatility (`rv10_partial90`), raw and divided by the median of the same minute over the previous 20 sessions (`__sadj`), each curve scaled by its median over minutes; LEGACY (2003–2018) and RAW (March 2022–2024) development data. The raw features show the intraday U-shape; the adjusted features are flat near 1.
