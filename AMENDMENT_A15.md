## Rules

1. Baseline tuning (5.2, 5.8; audit 2 finding 1). After greedy selection and the K/lambda tuning of the selected feature set, the return-only baseline of the same family is tuned on the fold's validation QLIKE with the identical grid (Gaussian and Student-t HMM K {2, 3, 4}; HSMM K {2, 3}; BOCPD lambda {12, 39, 78}), 3 starts and the same tie rule and budget as the feature tuning step. The baseline used in H1(a) and H1(b) scoring is that tuned baseline (`base_params`). The 5.13 final setups also carry the baseline parameters (mode across the era's folds, ties to the smaller value). Before: the baseline reused the feature model's K/lambda.
   - WHY: 5.2 gives the baseline the same hyperparameter budget; with the feature model's K the baseline had none, which biases both H1 contrasts towards the features. EVIDENCE: `section5_cold_audit_2.md` finding 1 (`s5_backend.py:99`).
2. Contamination: the first cold audit's runs (5.14; audit 2 finding 2). The audit-1 scripts are registered by name. `x5_robust.py` fitted all detectors on LEGACY fold 25's inner train and scored them on splices of its validation stable days (and did the same on LEGACY 29 and RAW 3): registered as touching LEGACY 25, RAW 3 and LEGACY 29, segments train and validation. `x3_events.py` and `x4_placebo.py` (RAW 3, LEGACY 29; train and validation) are registered likewise. `x1_folds.py`, `x1b.py` and `x2_causal.py` ran no detector and used no outcome: registered as label-free (rule 6d). The contamination list is regenerated. Expected: LEGACY 23 and 24 join the excluded folds (8 of 34 excluded, 26 confirmatory) and H1(c) keeps 98 eligible events; the regenerated numbers are recorded in the re-frozen protocol.
   - WHY: 5.14 counts a session touched when a model was fitted or a detector scored on it; that run informed A8 item 1 and was in no registry. EVIDENCE: audit 2 finding 2 (recomputation with the run registered gives 8 excluded folds); audit-1 scripts `x4_placebo.py:55-84`, `x5_robust.py:14-28`.
3. Registry check (5.14; audit 2 finding 3). The check that every data-loading script is registered uses a pattern without control characters that matches `fold_segments(`, `load_era(` and the construction of `DataBackend`, `EraBackend` and `MainBackend`. It lives in `s5_contamination.py` (`check_registry`), is called by the unit test, and a unit test shows it fails on a folder holding an unregistered data-loading script. `s5_a8_gate_seeds.py` (RAW 3, LEGACY 29; train and validation), `s5_a8_loo.py` (LEGACY 29; train and validation), `s5_machinery_run.py` (RAW 3, LEGACY 29; all segments) and `s5_a14_diag.py` (RAW 3, LEGACY 29; train) are registered; `s5_backend.py`, `s5_era_threshold.py` and test modules are libraries; the main run module is registered as the main run (it is the run the list protects, not a contamination).
   - WHY: the earlier test's pattern held two backspace bytes and matched no file, so it could not fail. EVIDENCE: audit 2 finding 3 (`s5_tests.py:413`, `od -c`).
4. Checkpoint version (5.16; audit 2 finding 4, audit 1 finding 11). The checkpoint version is the sha256 over a scheme tag 'A15' and the path and content hash of every result-affecting module: the transitive closure of local imports (in `section5/` and the repository root) from `s5_run.py`, `s5_backend.py`, `s5_era_threshold.py` and `s5_main.py`. The tag invalidates every older checkpoint. Checkpoint keys also carry the backend kind and the selection budget.
   - WHY: the old version hashed 7 files and missed the model, event, sweep and backend code. EVIDENCE: A12 and A13 machinery runs on RAW 3 share version `3807921739fee3b8` although A13 changed `s5_models.py`.
5. Main-run path (5.12, 5.13, 5.16; audit 2 finding 5). (a) `MainBackend` (`s5_main.py`) serves all 34 folds in consecutive order (LEGACY 0-29, RAW 0-3), one fold in memory at a time, sharing one era panel. (b) A fold that is not on the contamination list (a clean fold) is served only when the caller passes the sha256 of `SECTION5_PROTOCOL.md`, that hash equals the protocol's sidecar and the protocol states FROZEN; otherwise `PermissionError`. Contaminated folds stay available to machinery checks without it. (c) Freeze thresholds come from the A11 era-level rule only (`EraBackend.calibrate`, last 58 TRAIN sessions per era), through `era_thresholds`; the fold-level backend refuses it; the era calibration is refused until every fold has been selected and scored in the same run (it reads sessions of every fold's test segment). (d) `run_all` loads the contamination list itself (default `section5/results/contamination_list.json`; no exclusion argument) and records its sha256.
   - WHY: the only real-data backend refused every clean fold, and `freeze_step` would have frozen fold-validation thresholds. EVIDENCE: audit 2 finding 5 (`s5_backend.py:21-23,106-113`, `s5_run.py:58,74-78`).
6. (a) Holm (5.11): a missing family p-value (not evaluable) enters Holm as 1 and counts in m; it is never rejected. BH is unchanged. (b) H1(a) (5.6, 5.9): a fold with at least 8 stable test days whose splice construction fails is recorded as a failure with its reason (`a_failed`), not as a skip and not as a zero effect; it has no effect value, so it is not in the fold tests; failed folds are listed in the run summary and per family. (c) Freeze (5.12): the freeze records the sha256 of the protocol, of every amendment the protocol integrates, of the cited inputs, of the split manifest, of the contamination list and the H1(c) times, and of the LEGACY and RAW panel and SPY ETF files. (d) Contamination (5.14): an analysis that runs no detector or regime model and uses no outcome (session or stable-day counts, bar validity shares, splice specifications, feature or preprocessing computation) does not mark a session touched.
   - WHY: (a) dropping a family made Holm less strict than 'across the three families'; (b) an empty curve gave effect 0.0 with no record; (c) the freeze hashed only A6-A10 and the folds CSV; (d) read literally, the rule made every fold touched by the audit-1 stable-day counts. EVIDENCE: audit 2 findings 6, 7, 8, 9.
